# RakshaWell-AI: AI Safety & Ethical Architecture

## 1. Statutory Ethical Boundaries

1. **Non-Diagnostic Imperative**
   - The AI engine does NOT diagnose clinical depression, PTSD, anxiety disorders, or any psychiatric pathology.
   - Terminology is strictly operational: "Elevated Welfare Signal", "Change from Personal Baseline", "Sleep & Recovery Deficit", "Extended Duty Hours".

2. **Non-Disciplinary & Non-Evaluative Separation**
   - Welfare signals are strictly non-punitive.
   - Algorithms are mathematically isolated from deployment rosters, efficiency ratings, confidential reports, and promotion boards.

3. **Personal Baseline vs. Comparative Ranking**
   - An individual is evaluated solely against their OWN historical 12-month baseline.
   - Personnel are never ranked or benchmarked against peers to prevent peer competition or stigmatization.

4. **Transparent Explainability (SHAP Attributions)**
   - Every signal score is decomposed into positive and negative contributing factors with baseline comparisons.
   - Black-box scoring is strictly prohibited.

5. **Human Authority in the Loop**
   - The algorithm only recommends outreach; authorized human welfare officers make all substantive support determinations.
